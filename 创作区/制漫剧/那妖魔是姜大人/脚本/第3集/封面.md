## 中文封面 prompt

竖屏 9:16，冷月荒野官道，年轻女子姜月初身穿血污破损的镇魔司黑衣赤纹劲装，长发束成利落马尾，腰间挂横刀，神情冷硬但眼底藏着慌乱；她站在画面中央略偏前景，背后是十几名飞鹰门武人举着火把单膝跪地，火光照亮尘土和马匹轮廓。画面气氛：冒牌镇魔司大人被当成唯一希望，压迫、反差、悬疑。焦点清晰：黑衣赤纹、横刀、众人跪求。避免血腥猎奇，避免多人脸抢主角，避免现代服装，避免烤入大段文字。

## English cover prompt

Vertical 9:16 cinematic Chinese dark-fantasy scene on a moonlit wilderness road. Jiang Yuechu, a young woman, stands in the foreground wearing a blood-stained black-and-red Demon Suppression Bureau uniform, hair tied in a rough ponytail, a horizontal saber at her waist. Her expression is cold and authoritative, but her eyes hide panic. Behind her, more than a dozen martial men kneel with torches beside restless horses, treating her as their only hope. Strong contrast between false authority and desperate worship, tense and suspenseful atmosphere. Clear focus on the black-red uniform, saber, torchlight, kneeling crowd. No modern clothing, no excessive gore, no readable long text baked into the image.
