English Prompt: Vertical 9:16 cinematic realistic Northern Song market town in winter, natural skin and coarse linen textures, weathered timber and stone streets, cool overcast daylight contrasted with low warm stove light, motivated lighting, filmic depth and restrained colors, no fantasy glow, no modern objects, no Qing costumes, no text or watermark.

## 角色 / 生物

### CHAR_WUSONG 武松 @ 酒后战损 / 都头 / 搬离 / 出城

中文 Prompt：三十岁上下成年男性，身材高大结实但非夸张健美，眉骨硬朗、眼神沉着，短须，北宋平民武人发式；景阳冈态为磨损深褐短褐、尘土汗迹与断梢棒；都头态为整洁克制的宋代公服外搭与腰牌；搬离态背素布行李；同一脸型、发式、身高和服装主色，写实电影感，不仙侠化。

English Prompt: Adult male around thirty, tall and powerfully built without superhero exaggeration, strong brow, steady eyes, short beard, Northern Song martial commoner hairstyle; worn dark-brown travel clothes with dust and broken staff on Jingyang Ridge, restrained Song-era constable attire with badge later, plain cloth luggage when leaving; identical face, hairstyle, height and core colors across states, cinematic realism, no fantasy styling.

### CHAR_WUDA 武大 @ 卖饼 / 重逢 / 迟疑 / 送别

中文 Prompt：成年男性，身量矮小、肩背因挑担略弯，面相朴实温厚，冬日粗布短衣，手与肩有长期劳作痕迹；炊饼担是生活核心，不滑稽化，不丑角化，情绪从惊喜到迟疑、慌乱与担忧自然演进。

English Prompt: Adult short-statured male with slightly stooped shoulders from carrying a vendor pole, plain warm face, coarse winter work clothes, labor-worn hands and shoulder; bread pole as a livelihood anchor, never caricatured or comic, natural emotional progression from joy to hesitation, panic and worry.

### CHAR_PANJINLIAN 潘金莲 @ 压抑 / 试探 / 羞怒 / 隔帘

中文 Prompt：成年女性，端正清秀而非网红脸，北宋市民妇人发式与素净冬衣，主色灰白与暗青，眼神聪慧压抑；试探靠眼神、手势、距离和器物完成，不用低领暴露、艳情妆容或媚俗姿态；窗格与帘纱可形成克制遮挡。

English Prompt: Adult woman with refined natural features rather than an influencer face, Northern Song married-townswoman hairstyle and modest winter clothing in muted gray-white and dark teal, intelligent restrained eyes; desire shown through gaze, hand gesture, distance and objects, no revealing neckline, erotic makeup or vulgar posing; lattice and curtain may create restrained occlusion.

### BEAST_TIGER 景阳冈猛虎

中文 Prompt：成年华南虎体型与真实肌肉骨架，冬夜冷月侧逆光，毛发沾尘与枯叶，不怪兽化、不萌宠化；扑击、掀身、尾扫与战败状态保持同一花纹、体型和伤势，不夸张血浆。

English Prompt: Adult South China tiger with realistic anatomy and muscular weight, cold moonlit side rim, dusty fur and dry leaves, neither fantasy monster nor cute pet; identical stripe pattern, scale and injury state across pounce, rear, tail-sweep and defeated poses, no gore.

### CHAR_MAGISTRATE 知县 @ 公务态

中文 Prompt：成年男性县官，北宋地方官常服，案后端坐，克制威严，非清宫补服与官帽；只作为本集公务角色，不抢主角视觉重心。

English Prompt: Adult county magistrate in restrained Northern Song local official attire, seated behind a desk with quiet authority, explicitly not Qing court costume or mandarin badge robe, supporting role only.

### CHAR_XIMENQING 西门庆 @ 未揭面剪影态

中文 Prompt：成年男性，只露檐影下眼部、帽檐与持叉竿的手部轮廓，衣料比普通市民略讲究但不显示身份徽记，不露清晰全脸，不与潘金莲同框对话。

English Prompt: Adult man shown only as eyes beneath an eave shadow, hat brim and a hand holding a curtain pole, clothing subtly wealthier than common townsfolk but without identity marks, no clear full face, no dialogue or direct encounter with Pan Jinlian.

## 场景

### LOC_JINGYANGGANG 景阳冈夜间空地

中文 Prompt：北宋山冈冬夜，枯林、乱石、薄雾与泥土地，月光从画左上侧逆，冷青与土褐主色，竖屏纵深让虎从上方压下，武松位于下方，无仙侠雾光。

English Prompt: Northern Song mountain ridge at winter night, dry woodland, rocks, thin mist and dirt ground, moonlight from upper screen-left as cold side rim, cool cyan and earth-brown palette, vertical depth with tiger above and man below, no fantasy glow.

### LOC_YANGGU_STREET 阳谷街面与武大家楼窗

中文 Prompt：北宋县城窄街，青石、木铺、蒸汽、冬日漫射光；武大家为临街两层木屋，上有格窗与素帘，下有窄门，街面纵深清楚，避免清代牌楼和现代招牌。

English Prompt: Narrow Northern Song county street with stone paving, timber shops, cooking steam and overcast winter light; Wuda's modest two-story timber home has a lattice window and plain curtain above a narrow street door, clear depth, no Qing archways or modern signage.

### LOC_WUDA_HOME 武大家楼屋雪夜

中文 Prompt：低矮木屋室内，桌、长凳、炭盆、门闩与楼窗位置固定；窗雪冷光从画右后进入，炭火暖光从画左下托脸，潘固定画左、武松固定画右，桌沿和炭盆形成中央边界。

English Prompt: Low timber interior with fixed table, benches, brazier, door latch and upstairs window; cold snow light enters from rear screen-right while warm brazier light rises from lower screen-left, Pan locked screen-left and Wusong screen-right, table edge and brazier forming the center boundary.

### LOC_COUNTY_YAMEN 县衙案厅

English Prompt: Northern Song county yamen chamber with timber desk, document, seal and cool high-window light, restrained furnishings, magistrate screen-left behind desk and Wusong lower screen-right, no Qing elements or modern maps.

### LOC_CITY_GATE 阳谷城门清晨
